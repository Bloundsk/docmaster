**First, where the impact really is.** Most of the environmental cost of a phone
or a computer comes from manufacturing it, not from the electricity it uses —
and that cost is already paid the moment you switch it on. Deleting old emails
is a symbolic gesture that saves almost nothing; keeping your phone two more
years makes a real difference.

**Second, reading the figures.** When you stream video, the screen you watch on
matters far more than the video quality. A large television and a small phone
are worlds apart, while lowering the quality on that same television only
saves small change.

**Finally, design and the rebound effect.** Software so heavy that it forces
people to throw away hardware that still works is the worst hidden cost in
technology. And even as technology becomes more efficient, overall consumption
rises, because we simply use it more.

This path is education, not personal advice. The most powerful environmental
choice you can make with your devices is to keep them a little longer.
