*Written summary of the episode. The audio is produced by NotebookLM from the
learning path itself; this text is therefore not a word-for-word transcript,
it says the same thing in fewer words.*

**First, review early and test yourself.** Forgetting is at its harshest in the
first two days. Beware of the illusion of mastery: a text that reads easily is
not a text you know. Rather than rereading, test yourself and space out your
reviews. Rereading is looking at a recipe; testing yourself is cooking the meal
from memory.

**Second, mix things up and protect your sleep.** Mixing different kinds of
exercises, instead of doing a long series of the same one, trains you to choose
the right method, not just to apply it. And memory is consolidated at night:
sleep is working time for your brain, and an all-nighter is a bad trade.

**Finally, build a system that does not depend on motivation.** Motivation will
fade. Set a fixed time every day, and keep a five-minute version for the
impossible days, so the habit survives when everything else goes wrong.

This path is education, not personal or medical advice. Good learning feels a
little uncomfortable at first — that effort is exactly what makes it last.
