**First, know your levers.** Search ranking is slow, paid ads are instant but
expensive. The lever people underrate is email: a social network is like a
rented flat, where the platform sets the rules, while an email list is a home
you own, because the contacts are yours.

**Second, map the buying journey.** Take a simple four-step path: if half of
your visitors drop off at each step, only about six percent reach the end.
That is not a funnel, it is a leaky bucket. Fix the biggest leak before you pay
to pour more traffic into it.

**Finally, look at the blind spot: retention.** Everyone chases new customers,
yet keeping an existing one costs several times less, and a small improvement
in how many you keep makes a large difference to the results.

This path is education, not personal or legal advice. Winning at digital
marketing is not about spending the most: fix your leaks, own your audience,
and look after the customers you already have.
