How this page is made

Twice a day, an automated search goes through the online press for every section of every guide. It publishes nothing: it proposes.

Every article on this page has been read and selected by hand. An automated selection would also bring up promotional content and sloppy articles — the sorting is what makes the difference between a press review and a news feed.
