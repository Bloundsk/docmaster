About DocMaster

A free library of knowledge, built step by step.

The project

DocMaster is a library of free educational guides, from finance to health at work. The home page gives the complete list, each guide split into three levels.

Every guide starts from zero. No prior knowledge is needed: technical vocabulary is explained as it comes up, and the glossary gathers all the terms used on the site.

How the guides are built

A definition is not enough to understand something. Every notion therefore follows the same pattern:

  • an explanation that genuinely develops the subject;
  • a worked example, with numbers you can check;
  • the common mistake on that precise point, and what it costs;
  • a sentence to remember, and a quiz to check it has stuck.

Sections are collapsed by default: a course page runs between 1,400 and 2,200 words, and showing everything at once discourages more than it helps. You open what interests you.

The two languages

The site was written in French, and the fourteen course paths are fully translated into English — 56 pages, with their simulators and their quizzes.

Two things stay in French, on purpose. The news items point to French sources: a translated headline could no longer be found again. And anything that describes French law — tax wrappers, notice periods, legal forms — keeps its French figures, with a warning at the top of the page and, in the sentence itself, a note that the rule is French.

What runs on its own

Two mechanisms work by themselves. An automated watch goes through the news for every subsection of the guides each day and flags what would deserve an update. And the quiz questions change every two weeks, so that a guide read again later does not ask the same questions.

No account, no cookie, no advertising

The site sets no cookie. That is why you have never seen a consent banner on arriving. There is no advertising, no paid link and no sponsored content.

Your favourites, your progress and your choice of theme stay in your browser and are sent to no server. That is what makes it possible to offer a personal space without ever asking for a sign-up, a password or an email address.

The detail is in the legal notice.

Taking part

The site is hosted free of charge on GitHub Pages, and its code is public. If a subject is missing for you, the suggestion box lets you propose it in a few seconds — the requests that come back most often are handled first.

The most common questions are answered in the FAQ.
