📖 Glossary

Technical terms explained simply, across every subject.

💰 Finance
ETF (tracker)
A fund that replicates the performance of a whole stock market index, giving automatic diversification at low cost.
💰 Finance
Compound interest
The mechanism by which the gains on an investment themselves generate gains over time.
💰 Finance
PEA
Plan d'Épargne en Actions: a French wrapper for investing in shares, with lighter tax after 5 years. No exact equivalent outside France.
🤖 IA
LLM (Large Language Model)
A large language model trained on vast amounts of text to understand and generate human language.
🤖 IA
Hallucination
False information generated by an AI in a confident tone, as though it were true.
🤖 IA
Prompt engineering
The craft of writing clear, precise instructions for an AI in order to get better results.
💻 Web Development
DOM
The representation of the HTML page that JavaScript can read and change on the fly.
💻 Web Development
Responsive design
Building a site so that it displays correctly on every kind of screen.
📢 Marketing
SEO
Search Engine Optimization: techniques for improving a site's position in unpaid search results.
📢 Marketing
SEA / CPC
Paid advertising on search engines, usually billed per click (Cost Per Click).
🔒 Cybersecurity
Phishing
A scam technique that impersonates a trusted organisation in order to steal information.
🔒 Cybersecurity
2FA
Two-factor authentication: an extra layer of security on top of the password.
🚀 Entrepreneurship
Business plan
The document that structures a business project: offer, market, strategy and financial forecast.
🚀 Entrepreneurship
SASU / SARL
French company legal forms, which set the legal, tax and social framework of the project.
⏱️ Productivity
The Pomodoro method
A way of working in focused 25-minute sessions, separated by short breaks.
⏱️ Productivity
GTD
Getting Things Done: a method based on capturing every task in a reliable system outside your head.
📊 Data
KPI
Key Performance Indicator: the key measure of whether a goal is being met.
📊 Data
Correlation vs causation
Two things that move together are not necessarily linked by cause and effect.
🎨 Design
UX vs UI
UX is about the overall experience of using something; UI is about its concrete visual appearance.
🎨 Design
Wireframe
A plain, schematic mock-up of an interface, made before the final visual design.
