📖 Glossary

Technical terms explained simply, across every subject.

💰 Finance
ETF (tracker)
A fund that replicates the performance of a whole stock market index, giving automatic diversification at low cost.
💰 Finance
Compound interest
The mechanism by which the gains on an investment themselves generate gains over time.
💰 Finance
PEA
Plan d'Épargne en Actions: a French wrapper for investing in shares, with lighter tax after 5 years. No exact equivalent outside France.
🤖 IA
LLM (Large Language Model)
A large language model trained on vast amounts of text to understand and generate human language.
🤖 IA
Hallucination
False information generated by an AI in a confident tone, as though it were true.
🤖 IA
Prompt engineering
The craft of writing clear, precise instructions for an AI in order to get better results.
💻 Web Development
DOM
The representation of the HTML page that JavaScript can read and change on the fly.
💻 Web Development
Responsive design
Building a site so that it displays correctly on every kind of screen.
📢 Marketing
SEO
Search Engine Optimization: techniques for improving a site's position in unpaid search results.
📢 Marketing
SEA / CPC
Paid advertising on search engines, usually billed per click (Cost Per Click).
🔒 Cybersecurity
Phishing
A scam technique that impersonates a trusted organisation in order to steal information.
🔒 Cybersecurity
2FA
Two-factor authentication: an extra layer of security on top of the password.
🚀 Entrepreneurship
Business plan
The document that structures a business project: offer, market, strategy and financial forecast.
🚀 Entrepreneurship
SASU / SARL
French company legal forms, which set the legal, tax and social framework of the project.
⏱️ Productivity
The Pomodoro method
A way of working in focused 25-minute sessions, separated by short breaks.
⏱️ Productivity
GTD
Getting Things Done: a method based on capturing every task in a reliable system outside your head.
📊 Data
KPI
Key Performance Indicator: the key measure of whether a goal is being met.
📊 Data
Correlation vs causation
Two things that move together are not necessarily linked by cause and effect.
🎨 Design
UX vs UI
UX is about the overall experience of using something; UI is about its concrete visual appearance.
🎨 Design
Wireframe
A plain, schematic mock-up of an interface, made before the final visual design.
⚖️ Law
Limitation period
The deadline beyond which a right can no longer be claimed in court. In France it is 5 years in the general case; check your own country's.
⚖️ Law
Formal notice
A formal letter demanding that an obligation be met. It starts the clock on penalties and comes before any court action.
⚖️ Law
GDPR
The European regulation on personal data. It grants rights of access, rectification and erasure, valid throughout the Union.
🩺 Health at Work
Risk assessment record
The inventory of occupational risks every French employer must keep up to date, whatever the size of the company.
🩺 Health at Work
Mental load
The cognitive effort a task demands: remembering, deciding, reorganising. It tires without leaving any visible trace.
🩺 Health at Work
Right to withdraw
The ability to stop work in the face of serious and imminent danger, without loss of pay or sanction. A French statutory right.
🌱 Digital Sustainability
Rebound effect
A gain in efficiency is absorbed by greater use: a device that consumes half as much but is used three times as often consumes more.
🌱 Digital Sustainability
Eco-design
Designing a digital service to consume less: less data transferred, less computation, and hardware that lasts.
🌱 Digital Sustainability
Manufacturing footprint
The share of a device's impact caused by making it, before any use. For a phone it far outweighs the electricity it will ever consume.
🤝 Negotiation
BATNA
Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement: what you will do if the deal fails. It sets your real leverage, not your confidence.
🤝 Negotiation
Anchoring
The first figure named steers the whole discussion that follows, even when everyone knows it is arbitrary.
🤝 Negotiation
Active listening
Restating what the other person said to check you understood it. It surfaces disagreements both sides believed were settled.
🎓 Learning
Spaced repetition
Revising at growing intervals, just before you would forget. It is the method whose effectiveness is best established.
🎓 Learning
Forgetting curve
A description of how fast something learned fades without being reactivated — very fast in the first few days.
🎓 Learning
Active recall
Forcing yourself to retrieve information from memory rather than rereading it. More uncomfortable than rereading, and far more effective.
