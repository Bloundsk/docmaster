Personal data

The site is designed to keep the data processed to a strict minimum. Three separate processing operations coexist, described below.

1. Settings kept in your browser

The display theme, the search history, reading progress and your favourites use your browser's local storage (localStorage). This information stays on your device, is sent to no server, and disappears when you clear your browsing data.

That is why the site offers no sign-up: no email address, no password, no user account. The My space page merely displays what your own browser has recorded, and lets you erase all of it in one click.

2. Audience measurement

The site uses GoatCounter to measure its traffic. This tool sets no cookie, keeps no IP address, and makes it possible neither to identify you nor to follow you from one site to another. Only aggregated data is kept: pages viewed, referring site, country, browser type.

On the same basis and through the same tool, the site counts three actions inside the guides: opening a section, adding a favourite, and marking a guide as read. This counting serves to know which subjects are of most interest, in order to guide the next guides.

Only the name of the action is transmitted — for example “section opened: ETFs”. No identifier, no cookie, nothing that would allow two actions to be linked to the same person. These measurements do not leave GoatCounter and are never cross-referenced with the contents of your favourites, which stay in your browser and are transmitted to nobody.

On that basis, this arrangement falls under audience measurement exempt from consent within the meaning of the recommendations of the CNIL, the French data protection authority.

3. Host logs

The IP addresses recorded by GitHub Pages are the host's responsibility, as indicated in the previous section.

Suggestion box

You are asked for no address: the form contains only the subject proposed and, if you wish, the reason it interests you. You therefore remain anonymous, unless you choose to identify yourself in the text of your message.
