❓ Frequently asked questions

Everything worth knowing about Clicked, in short answers.

The site

What is Clicked?

A library of free educational guides, from finance to health at work. The Guides page gives the complete list — it is the only place kept up to date.

Every guide starts from zero and gets to the point: the key ideas, concrete examples, and a quiz to check you have understood.

Is the site really free?

Yes, entirely. No sign-up, no subscription, no paid tier, no advertising. Nothing is reserved for members.

The site is hosted free of charge by GitHub Pages, which is what makes it possible to charge nothing.

Who is Clicked for?

Beginners and the curious. No prior knowledge is needed: the guides explain the vocabulary as they go, and the glossary gathers all the technical terms.

If you are already an expert in one area, that guide will probably look too simple to you — but the others may still interest you.

Do I need to create an account?

No, and it is not even possible. The site manages no user accounts: no email address, no password.

Your favourites, your reading progress and your choice of theme are saved directly in your browser, without passing through a server. The My space page shows them to you.

It is a deliberate choice: asking for a sign-up just for bookmarks would mean storing passwords, with every risk that entails.

How do I bookmark a guide or a section?

Click the star ☆. You will find one on every card of the Guides page, at the top of every guide, and to the right of every section title.

The star turns gold ★ once the favourite is saved. You will find them all in My space, along with your progress.

Do my favourites follow me from one device to another?

No. They are saved in the browser you are using, on this particular device.

If you change computer or phone, or if you clear your browsing data, they start again from zero. That is the direct counterpart of having no account: nothing is stored on a server, so nothing can be synchronised — nor read by anybody.

The content

In what order should I read the guides?

Whatever order you like: each guide stands on its own and does not assume you have read the others.

If you are unsure, every guide offers “related guides” at the bottom of the page, along with “previous / next” links that suggest a coherent route.

Are the guides up to date?

The guides cover fundamentals, which change slowly. Every page shows its date of last update, calculated automatically from the real changes to the content.

A watch also runs twice a day over every section of every guide. The most interesting things it brings back are published on the News page.

That said, for anything touching on figures, rates, prices or regulation, always check the information at its original source.

Where do the podcasts come from?

Every learning path has its episode, about a minute and a half long. It is produced automatically from the text of the path: the voice is synthetic, nobody reads these episodes aloud.

That is what makes them possible. Fourteen readings would take hours, to be redone every time a guide is corrected. The guide texts themselves are written by hand.

An episode is a summary, not a replacement: it gives the key idea and the order of the steps, but the calculations, simulators and quizzes have no spoken equivalent. Each episode comes with a written summary, which is not a word-for-word transcript.

The episodes are in French. They play at the top of each learning path, on the Podcasts page, or in any podcast app through the feed.

Where do the articles on the News page come from?

An automated search goes through the online press twice a day, one query per guide section, and publishes what it finds without any human step.

A filter removes rankings, affiliate comparisons and press releases. It cannot judge whether an article is accurate, only whether it looks like advertising — so an article published here has not been proofread. Anything unwanted is taken down as soon as it is noticed.

Every article is attached to the guide it extends, so you can go from the news item to the underlying idea in one click.

These articles are published on other sites: citing them means they seemed worth reading, not that their content is endorsed or guaranteed accurate. They are in French, like the sources they come from.

Can I follow the financial advice in the Finance guide?

The Finance guide is educational content. It explains how saving, ETFs, diversification and risk work — it recommends no particular investment.

It is not personalised investment advice, and it does not replace a regulated adviser. No investment is without risk, and past performance does not predict future performance. Note also that the tax wrappers it describes are French.

Can I propose a new subject or category?

Yes, and you are encouraged to. The suggestion box lets you propose either a whole area or one precise subject to add to an existing guide.

The requests that come back most often are handled first. The page also shows the themes already under consideration, which you can give your view on.

I found a mistake — how do I report it?

By email to docmaster.contact [arobase] proton.me — the same address to use for any request concerning your personal data.

Please say which guide and section if you can: that is what makes the fastest correction possible.

Can I reuse the content?

For personal or educational use — revising, quoting a definition, leaning on a guide to explain something to somebody — no problem at all.

For full republication or commercial use, write to docmaster.contact [arobase] proton.me to discuss it.

Using the site

Why are the guide sections collapsed?

A course page runs between 1,400 and 2,200 words. Showing it all at once presents a wall of text that discourages before you have even started.

Collapsed, the guide fits on one screen: you see its structure at a glance and open what interests you. The “Expand all” button opens everything if you prefer to read straight through.

Clicking an entry in the contents automatically opens the section it points to — you do not have to hunt for it.

How do I search the guides quickly?

Press the / key from any page: the cursor goes straight into the search bar.

The search tolerates typos. Its entries are titled in French, because it indexes the original version — but the links lead to the English pages.

How do I switch between light and dark theme?

By default the site follows your device setting: if your phone or computer is in dark mode, so is the site. There is nothing for you to do.

To force a theme, click the 🌙 or ☀️ icon at the top right. Your choice then takes priority over the system setting, and it is remembered for your next visits in this browser.

To go back to following the system, clear the site data in your browser settings.

What is the “I have read and understood this guide” box for?

To track your progress. Once ticked, the guide is marked as finished and stays that way on your next visits.

It is a personal reminder: nobody else sees this information, and it is sent nowhere.

Do I find my progress again on another device?

No. Progress is stored in the browser you are using, on this particular device.

If you change computer, phone or browser, or if you clear your browsing data, progress starts again from zero. That is the counterpart of the choice to create no user accounts.

Are the quizzes graded?

No, and no result is recorded. The quiz is only there to make you think: you see immediately whether your answer is right or wrong, with an explanation.

You can retake it as many times as you like.

There is a quiz at the bottom of each section, plus a summary quiz at the end of the guide whose questions are different and cover the whole subject.

Why do the questions change?

Every quiz draws on a larger pool than it shows. Every two weeks, it switches automatically to other questions.

The point: coming back to a guide a few weeks later does not ask you exactly the same questions, which tests that you understood rather than memorised.

Reloading the page changes nothing: everybody sees the same set at the same time. Otherwise it would be enough to refresh until the familiar questions came up.

Can I print a guide or read it offline?

Yes. Press Ctrl + P: a print-specific layout removes the navigation, the buttons and the interactive elements, keeping only the text.

You can also choose “Save as PDF” rather than a printer.

Does the site work on a phone?

Yes, the layout adapts to phone and tablet screens: the navigation reorganises itself and the text stays readable without zooming.

Data and privacy

Does the site use cookies?

None, without exception. That is why you have never seen an “accept cookies” banner on arriving.

The display theme, your favourites and your reading progress use the browser's local storage (localStorage), which stays on your device and is sent to no server.

The suggestion box sends your suggestion directly from the page, without asking for an address and without loading any verification system.

What data is collected?

Only aggregated traffic statistics, through a privacy-respecting tool: pages viewed, the site the visitor came from, country and browser type.

To that is added the counting of three actions inside the guides: opening a section, adding a favourite, marking a guide as read. This serves to know which subjects are of most interest, in order to decide the next guides. Only the name of the action is sent — “section opened: ETFs” — with nothing that would allow two actions to be linked to the same person.

No IP address is kept, and no identifier makes it possible to recognise you from one visit to the next or to follow you on other sites. The contents of your favourites stay in your browser: what is counted is that a section was bookmarked, never by whom nor alongside what else.

The only exception is what you write yourself in the suggestion box. A suggestion is kept for 24 months at most, long enough to see whether the subject comes back often, then it is deleted. As you are asked for no address, it stays anonymous — unless you identify yourself in the text.

The detail is in the legal notice.

Is there advertising or sponsored content?

No. No advertising, no affiliate links, no paid partnerships. The site pursues no profit, which guarantees that nothing is promoted for commercial reasons.
