⏱️ Productivity & Organisation

Organising yourself better to get more done, day to day.

🕒 Last updated: 16 August 2026

The path

Three levels, to be followed in order or picked up wherever you stand. Each reads on its own, but each assumes what came before.

  1. Level 1 Beginner The four methods that have proved themselves. No prior knowledge needed.
    • The Pomodoro technique
    • The GTD method
    • The Eisenhower matrix
    • Task management tools

    4 sections · about 10 minutes · no prior knowledge needed

  2. Level 2 Intermediate Protecting your attention rather than filling your calendar.
    • The cost of task switching
    • Time blocking
    • Handling incoming streams
    • Prioritising for real

    4 sections · about 12 minutes · assumes level 1

  3. Level 3 Advanced What lasts for years, and does not depend on motivation.
    • Energy rather than time
    • Systems rather than willpower
    • Collective productivity
    • Stepping back regularly

    4 sections · about 13 minutes · assumes levels 1 and 2

⚡ What you will know by the end

  • Why nine three-minute interruptions cost two and a quarter hours.
  • How much time you really have left for deep work, once everything is deducted.
  • Why handling your messages in two passes saves hours a day.
  • How to refuse without closing a door, by making the cost visible.
  • Why a one-hour meeting with eight people consumes a working day.

Before you start

Warning

This path is educational. The durations and rates quoted are orders of magnitude meant to convey a mechanism: they vary with people and with jobs.

Persistent tiredness, sleep problems or exhaustion are not matters of organisation. They warrant medical advice, which this guide does not replace.
