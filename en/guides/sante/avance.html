⚡ Key points to remember

  • In France the occupational doctor is not the employer's doctor: the visit is a right, and confidential.
  • The duty of safety bears on prevention, not on redress after the harm.
  • Burnout has three described dimensions — and no online test can conclude anything.
  • Facing a serious situation, writing the facts down and dating them comes before everything else.

Introduction

This is the level most useful to read before you need it. These mechanisms are hard to discover while in the middle of a situation.

Warning — please read

This guide is educational and describes French law. Occupational medicine, the single risk assessment document, the works council, the right of withdrawal, the shifted burden of proof: these are French mechanisms. Most countries have equivalents, but they differ — check yours.

It makes no diagnosis and replaces neither a doctor, nor a lawyer, nor an employee representative.

The exercises on this page are about procedures and knowledge — never about your state of health. None returns a score, and none concludes anything about you.

If you are unwell: speak to your own doctor or an occupational doctor. If you are in distress, contact your national crisis line; in France, 3114 answers free of charge, 24 hours a day.

The actors and the remedies

🏥 Occupational medicine

This is the most powerful lever in the field, and one of the most misunderstood. The most widespread confusion: believing the occupational doctor works for the employer.

What they are, and are not

The occupational doctor is a doctor, bound by medical confidentiality like any other. The employer who pays them has access neither to what you say nor to your file — only to the conclusions on your fitness for the job.

Their role is exclusively preventive: they do not treat, they prevent work from damaging health.

The visit at your request

This is the most overlooked point: any employee can ask for a visit, at any time, without going through their employer and without having to justify it. The employer cannot object, and cannot learn the reason.

During a long sick leave, a pre-return visit can be requested: it serves to prepare the return — adjustments, phased return, redeployment — before the return takes place.

What they can obtain

An adjustment to the job. Their proposals bind the employer, who can only set them aside by demonstrating impossibility.

A study of the job and the working conditions, on site.

An alert about a group: if they find a risk affecting several employees, they can report it in writing to the employer, who must respond.

None of these actions assumes you are ill. They only assume that something at work is a problem.

Common mistake — waiting for the periodic check-up

Periodic visits are several years apart in many situations. Waiting for the next one to raise a problem often means waiting too long.

The visit on request exists for exactly that, and it costs nothing — neither to the employee nor in holiday: it takes place during working hours.

💡 A visit at your request, confidential, with no reason to give, during working hours. It is a right, and it is very little used.

🛡️ The employer's duties

Health at work is not an optional internal policy: it is a legal duty, and its scope is wider than generally believed.

A duty of prevention

The employer must take the measures necessary to ensure safety and protect the physical and mental health of workers. Mental health is explicitly named in the French labour code.

That duty bears on prevention: assess the risks, avoid them at source, adapt the work to the person. Making good after the harm has occurred is not enough to discharge it.

The single document

The single risk assessment document is compulsory from the first employee. It lists the risks by work unit, and must include psychosocial risks, not only physical ones.

It is accessible to employees. Asking for it is legitimate, and no answer is itself information.

Who to turn to, in order

The works council — in France, in companies with at least eleven employees. Its members can raise alarms, investigate, and hold a right of alert in cases of serious and imminent danger.

The occupational health and prevention service, reachable directly.

The labour inspectorate, when nothing moves. Referral is free and can remain confidential on request.

The right of withdrawal

Faced with a serious and imminent danger, any employee may withdraw from the situation, having alerted the employer. No penalty or pay deduction may be applied on that ground alone.

Two conditions matter: the danger must be serious and imminent, and the withdrawal must not create a danger for others. The grounds are judged on what could reasonably be believed at the time.

Common mistake — believing nothing is owed without a works council

The duty of safety and the single document exist whatever the size of the company, including with no employee representatives.

In their absence, the occupational health service and the labour inspectorate remain directly reachable.

💡 The duty bears on prevention. An employer who makes good after the fact has not done what the law asks.

🔥 Burnout

The word has entered everyday language, which has made it both familiar and vague. It designates something precise, and it is not a synonym for tiredness or overwork.

The three described dimensions

The literature distinguishes three, which combine:

  1. Exhaustion — a tiredness that no longer yields to rest, physical as much as mental.
  2. Detachment — a distancing, sometimes cynicism, towards the work and towards people.
  3. Loss of the sense of efficacy — the feeling of no longer succeeding at anything, regardless of actual results.

It is not a medical diagnosis in the strict sense, and it does not appear as a standalone illness in the international classifications: it is described there as a work-related phenomenon. Which takes nothing away from its reality or its seriousness.

What separates it from a difficult period

Duration. Several weeks, with no improvement between periods.

The absence of recovery. The weekend, then holidays, are no longer enough.

Settling in. It is no longer a reaction to an event, it has become the baseline state.

One sign recurs constantly in accounts: the people around noticed before the person concerned. Gradual adaptation erases the points of comparison.

Why no online test will do

Validated questionnaires exist, and they are used by professionals — within a framework, with an interview, and with interpretation. Taken out of that framework, they produce numbers without meaning.

A score returned by a web page can wrongly reassure somebody who is unwell, or lastingly worry somebody who is fine. Neither error is acceptable, and that is why this page offers none.

The useful next step is always the same: talk to your own doctor or to an occupational doctor. Both are bound by confidentiality.

Common mistake — reading it as an individual weakness

Organisational factors — lasting overload, no room to manoeuvre, lack of recognition, conflicts of values — are documented and decisive.

Treating the question as a failure of personal resilience leaves the cause in place, and makes the person carry what belongs to the organisation.

💡 These three dimensions are there to name, not to conclude. Naming makes it possible to talk about it — that is already a great deal.

🚨 Serious situations

Bullying, sexual harassment, discrimination, violence: these are situations where the order of steps counts as much as their content. And where whatever was not written down at the time is very hard to establish afterwards.

What the law defines

Under French law, moral harassment is characterised by repeated acts whose object or effect is a deterioration of working conditions liable to harm rights, dignity, health or professional future.

Two points often unknown: intent is not required — the effect is enough; and nor is a reporting line — harassment between colleagues is made out in the same way.

Sexual harassment follows a distinct definition, and a single act is enough in some cases.

The order that counts

1. Write. The facts, dated, as they happen. It is the first thing everybody will ask for, and the hardest to reconstruct six months later.

2. Keep. Messages, emails, documents, rotas — anything that evidences the facts.

3. Alert in writing the employer or HR. It is that alert which triggers the duty to act: an employer informed and doing nothing engages its liability.

4. Involve the channels — employee representatives, harassment officer, occupational doctor, labour inspectorate, the national equality body.

The protections

An employee who reports or gives evidence of harassment cannot be penalised on that ground. A dismissal based on it is void.

In harassment and discrimination cases the burden of proof is shifted: the employee presents facts suggesting the situation, and it falls to the employer to show they are explained otherwise. That is a considerable difference from the ordinary rule — and one more reason to build a file.

Common mistake — waiting until you are sure of the label

Many hesitate to raise an alarm because they do not know whether the situation “counts as” harassment. It is not for the employee to categorise it.

Describing dated facts to a competent person is the useful step. The legal characterisation comes afterwards, and it is somebody else's job.

💡 Write the facts down, dated, from the start. It is what makes everything that follows possible, and nothing replaces it afterwards.

📚 Going further

  • Your national crisis line — free and confidential, around the clock in most countries. In France, 3114.
  • Your occupational health service — in France the visit at your request is a right, confidential and during working hours.
  • Your national occupational safety institute — material on harassment, psychosocial risks and the duty of prevention.
  • Your national equality body — free referral for discrimination and harassment.
  • Your labour inspectorate — free referral, confidential on request, including with no employee representatives.

One useful habit: note down today the contact details of your occupational health service. The moment you need them is not the moment you have the energy to look for them.
