💻 Web Development

Learning to build modern websites and web applications.

🕒 Last updated: 16 August 2026

The path

Three levels, to be followed in order or picked up wherever you stand. Each reads on its own, but each assumes what came before.

  1. Level 1 Beginner Understanding what a web page is made of. No programming background needed.
    • HTML and CSS: structure and appearance
    • JavaScript: what it brings, and what it costs
    • Browser or server: who does what
    • Adapting to every screen

    4 sections · about 9 minutes · no prior knowledge needed

  2. Level 2 Intermediate Making the pieces talk to each other, storing data, and going live.
    • APIs: a contract, not just an address
    • Databases and the trap of queries in a loop
    • Going live: the four points that catch people out
    • Git and working with others

    4 sections · about 12 minutes · assumes level 1

  3. Level 3 Advanced What separates a site that works from a site that lasts.
    • Performance, and caching as the only free lever
    • The three attacks to know, and their defences
    • Tests that make change possible
    • Technical debt, counted in hours

    4 sections · about 13 minutes · assumes levels 1 and 2

⚡ What you will know by the end

  • Why a page's timing depends on the number of round trips, not on the server's speed.
  • How one query placed inside a loop makes a page unusable.
  • What must be checked on the server without exception, and why.
  • How caching improves performance without slimming anything down.
  • How to put a figure on technical debt in order to get the time to fix it.

Before you start

Warning

This path is educational and independent of fashionable technologies. The principles set out here hold whatever the language or framework: tools change, mechanisms much less.

The vulnerabilities described at the advanced level are there to be understood and avoided from the design stage onwards.
