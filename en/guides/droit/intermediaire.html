Housing, work, self-employment: what is at stake when you sign.

⚡ Key points to remember

  • The check-in inventory is the one document that protects the deposit.
  • A deposit returned late is increased by 10% of the rent per month — as of right.
  • Notice runs from receipt, and a shortened notice must be justified in writing.
  • A probation period is never renewed tacitly.
  • Under the micro-entrepreneur regime costs are not deducted: the allowance is a flat rate.

Introduction

The beginner level covered reflexes that hold anywhere: search, count, write, keep. This one applies them to the three situations almost everybody meets — finding a home, working, registering a business.

What those three moments have in common: everything is decided at signature, and nothing is recovered afterwards without evidence.

Warning

This guide is about French law and it is educational. The rules described are those of the general case: many situations fall under special regimes — social housing, holiday lets, sector agreements, subsidised contracts. Outside France, none of these figures applies: check your own rules.

No amount or threshold is quoted here, because they change: refer to the official French portal, which is kept up to date.

For a dismissal, a tenancy dispute or setting up a company, consult a professional. Free consultations exist.

Three situations, and what is at stake

🏠 Renting a home

2 min A tenancy agreement is a heavily regulated contract: the law imposes its content, caps the deposit and sets the deadlines.

A tenancy agreement is a heavily regulated contract: the law imposes its content, caps the deposit and sets the deadlines. Much of what the parties “agree” has no value at all.

What must appear, and what cannot

The agreement must be in writing and carry a set of compulsory particulars — identity of the parties, description of the property, rent and charges, term. The technical survey file and an information notice must be attached.

Conversely, some clauses are prohibited and deemed unwritten, even signed: imposing a single method of payment, forbidding you to host a relative, making the tenant bear repairs that fall to the landlord.

The inventory, the key document

It is the only document that will let you distinguish, on leaving, what you damaged from what was already damaged. With no check-in inventory, the property is presumed to have been handed over in good condition — and the burden turns against you.

Take the time: note every mark, photograph, read the meters. Twenty minutes on arrival are worth several hundred euros on departure.

Worked example — a €700 deposit returned 3 months late

Rent excluding charges: €700. No justified deductions.

Amount owed to you: €700. Legal deadline: 1 month if the check-out inventory matches the check-in one, 2 months otherwise.

Late-payment increase: €210 — 10% of the monthly rent excluding charges per month started.

Total claimable: €910. That increase is owed as of right; it is not negotiated, it is claimed.

Common mistake — accepting an unjustified deduction

The landlord may only retain what they can justify: a quote, an invoice, or a joint report. “Making good: €400” with nothing attached is not enough.

They must also allow for wear and tear: a ten-year-old carpet cannot be charged at the price of a new one.

💡 The check-in inventory is the cheapest document to draw up and the most expensive not to have.

🚪 Giving notice

2 min Leaving a property follows simple rules, where almost every mistake costs a month's rent.

Leaving a property follows simple rules, where almost every mistake costs a month's rent.

The length of the notice

Unfurnished tenancy: three months. Furnished tenancy: one month. Notice on an unfurnished tenancy drops to one month in several cases: property in a designated tight market, job transfer, job loss, obtaining a first job, health justifying a move, receipt of certain benefits.

One decisive point: the reason must be stated in the notice letter. With no written reason the notice stays three months — even if you met the condition.

The starting point

Notice runs from the landlord's receipt of the letter, not from posting it. Two days in the post can shift the leaving date, and therefore a whole month's rent.

Notice is given by recorded delivery with proof of receipt, by a judicial officer, or handed over against signature. An email is not enough.

What happens during the notice period

Rent remains due until the end, unless the property is re-let before then with the landlord's agreement — in which case rent stops being due from the new tenancy.

The landlord may show the property, but in agreed slots: they do not hold the keys and cannot impose weekends and public holidays.

The check-out inventory

It is done when the keys are handed back, in your presence — that is what makes the line-by-line comparison with the check-in one possible. An inventory made in your absence carries far less weight.

Read the meters, keep a signed copy, and photograph the empty property with a date. That is what will make a challenge possible rather than theoretical.

Common mistake — leaving without giving an address

The landlord must return the deposit to your new address. Without it, the deadline does not run usefully and the return bogs down.

State it in the notice letter, and repeat it on the check-out inventory.

💡 A shortened notice with no written reason is a three-month notice. The missing sentence costs two months' rent.

💼 The employment contract

2 min The employment contract is the area where French law leaves the least room for the parties' agreement: the essentials are imposed, whatever was signed.

The employment contract is the area where French law leaves the least room for the parties' agreement: the essentials are imposed, whatever was signed.

Permanent, fixed-term: the rule and the exception

The permanent contract is the normal form of employment. A fixed-term contract is an exception, possible only in an exhaustive list of cases — cover for an absent employee, a temporary increase in activity, seasonal work — and it must state its reason in writing.

A fixed-term contract with no writing, or with no valid reason, can be reclassified as permanent. That is not theoretical: it is one of the most frequent employment disputes.

The probation period

It lets either side end the contract with no reason and no compensation. Its maximum length on a permanent contract depends on the category: 2 months for manual and clerical staff, 3 months for supervisors and technicians, 4 months for managers.

It can be renewed once — but only if a sector agreement provides for it, if the contract mentions it, and with your written agreement. It is never renewed tacitly.

Ending probation requires a notice period from the employer, which grows with length of service: 24 hours before 8 days of service, 48 hours between 8 days and 1 month, 2 weeks after 1 month, 1 month after 3 months.

Worked example — manager, 4-month probation, 45 days served

End of the probation period: in 77 days.

Maximum length with renewal: 8 months — and only if the contract provides for it.

Notice period applicable today: 2 weeks.

An employer ending probation without observing that period owes compensation in lieu — the ending remains valid, but it is paid for.

The sector agreement

This is the great forgotten one. It applies on top of the labour code and very often provides better: grading, sector minimum pay, notice, bonuses, days for family events.

Its name appears on the payslip. Reading it once — at least the articles on notice and grading — pays off more than a good deal of legal research.

Common mistake — signing a non-compete clause with no consideration

A non-compete clause is valid only if it is limited in time and space, justified by the company's interest, and accompanied by financial consideration.

Without consideration it is void. Many contracts still contain one.

💡 Whatever the contract provides that is less favourable than the law or the sector agreement does not apply. Signing changes nothing.

🧾 Registering a business

2 min Any regular, profit-making activity must be registered, however modest, however occasional.

Any regular, profit-making activity must be registered, however modest, however occasional. The question is therefore not whether to register, but under which form.

The micro-entrepreneur regime, and what it assumes

It is the simplest: online registration, contributions calculated as a percentage of turnover, no VAT below a certain threshold, accounts reduced to a record of receipts.

Its central mechanism is often misunderstood: actual costs are not deductible. The tax authority applies a flat-rate allowance supposed to represent them. A low-expense activity gains from it; an activity buying a lot of materials or equipment loses, whatever its turnover.

The turnover thresholds and contribution rates are revised regularly: check them on the official French sites rather than in an article, this one included.

The calculation that decides

Two businesses with the same turnover. The first sells consultancy: almost no expenses. The second makes objects: raw materials account for a substantial share of receipts.

Under this regime both are taxed the same way — on turnover less a flat-rate allowance. The first does well out of it; the second pays tax on money it never earned.

That is the only question that counts when choosing: what share of my receipts goes out in expenses?

The other forms, one line each

  • Sole trader on actual accounts — same formalities, but costs are genuinely deducted. For a high-expense activity.
  • Single-member company — heavier to run, but it separates your assets and lets partners join later.
  • Non-profit association — for an activity with no distribution of profits. It is not a way of escaping tax rules if the activity is in reality profit-making.

Chambers of commerce and trade offer free appointments to help decide. An hour with an adviser beats a choice made on a forum.

Common mistake — believing nothing is owed below a certain amount

There is no threshold below which a regular activity escapes registration. Even nil turnover has to be declared.

What does exist are simpler regimes depending on the amount — hence the confusion.

💡 Under this regime you are taxed on what you take in, not on what you earn. The whole decision rests there.

📚 Going further

  • Your national housing information agency — in France, ANIL: sheets, model letters and free consultations by department.
  • Official labour-law tools — in France, the “code du travail numérique” answers according to your sector agreement, not only the statute.
  • Your social security collection agency — thresholds, contribution rates and official calculators, kept up to date.
  • Chambers of commerce and trade — free appointments to help choose a legal form.

One useful habit: find the name of your sector agreement on your payslip, then read its articles on notice and grading. Ten minutes that often reveal rights nobody mentioned.
