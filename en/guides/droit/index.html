⚖️ Law & Procedures

Knowing where to look, how long is left, and what to write.

🕒 Last updated: 17 August 2026

Before you start

Warning — please read

This path is about French law and it is educational. Every period, every remedy and every procedure described here is French: they will not be the same where you are. Read it as an example of how a legal system fits together, and check your own country's rules before acting.

It is not legal advice: it knows neither your situation, nor your contract, nor the facts.

The periods quoted are statutory ones, stable for years. By contrast, no amount, threshold or ceiling appears in it: those values are revised regularly, and a stale figure would be worse than no figure. The guide points to the official source each time.

For anything that commits you — dismissal, a significant dispute, a notarised deed — consult a professional. In France, points-justice give free legal consultations, and legal aid can cover costs depending on your means; most countries have an equivalent.
