📢 Digital Marketing — Beginner

Understanding the four basic levers. No prior knowledge needed.

🕒 Last updated: 5 September 2026

📑 Contents

  • Introduction
  • 🔍 SEO (search ranking)
  • 💰 Online advertising (paid search)
  • 📱 Social networks
  • ✉️ Email marketing

⚡ Key points to remember

  • SEO is free but slow, paid search costs money but is immediate.
  • Choose the right social platform rather than being everywhere with no plan.
  • An email list belongs to you, unlike an audience on a social network.
  • Respecting consent preserves the brand's trustworthiness.
  • SEO rests on three pillars: content, technical, popularity.

Introduction

Digital marketing has an ambiguous reputation, partly deserved: it is a field where you can spend a great deal very fast, with no result, while producing reassuring reports.

Its real peculiarity lies elsewhere: unlike billboards or radio, almost everything in it is measurable. You can know what an acquired customer costs, and compare it to what they bring in. That comparison is the only valid judge — and, paradoxically, the one many people avoid making.

This guide covers the four main levers, with for each what it really costs, how much time it takes, and the situations where it does not fit.

The basics to know

🔍 SEO (search ranking)

2 min SEO aims at appearing in a search engine's unpaid results.

SEO aims at appearing in a search engine's unpaid results. Its strength: the traffic obtained does not stop when you stop paying. Its weakness: it takes months before you see anything.

The three pillars

  1. Content — genuinely answering the question asked. That is 70% of the work.
  2. Technical — a fast site, readable on mobile, on HTTPS, with no dead pages.
  3. Popularity — the links pointing at you from other sites, which act as recommendations.

Search intent

This is the most useful notion, and the most ignored. Behind every query sits a different expectation:

QueryIntentExpected content
“what is an ETF”UnderstandAn explanatory article
“best ETF for a stocks and shares ISA”CompareAn argued comparison
“open a stocks and shares ISA”ActA sign-up page

Offering a sales page to somebody who wants to understand drives them away. Search engines measure that departure and draw their conclusions.

The calculation that directs the effort

Two keywords targeted by a brand-new site:

  • “car insurance” — 100,000 searches a month, but a sector where the established players invest millions. Probability of appearing: near zero.
  • “car insurance young driver with claims history Leeds” — 90 searches a month, almost no competition, and visitors very close to buying.

90 qualified visitors are worth infinitely more than 0 visitors on an unreachable keyword. Long, specific queries — the “long tail” — are the only realistic way in at the start.

Common mistake — writing for the algorithm

Repeating a keyword, artificially padding a text, producing ten articles that look alike: those techniques worked fifteen years ago. Today they are detected and penalised.

The criterion that remains: does the visitor find their answer and leave satisfied? Everything else follows from that.

💡 Allow 6 to 12 months before serious results. If a contractor promises first place in three weeks, the matter is settled.

💰 Online advertising (paid search)

2 min Online advertising buys immediate visibility.

Online advertising buys immediate visibility. It is the exact complement of SEO: instant, measurable, and stopping dead as soon as the budget runs out.

How the auction works

Contrary to a common belief, the highest bidder does not win. Platforms combine the bid amount with a quality score: relevance of the ad, quality of the landing page, expected click rate.

The consequence: a well-designed ad costs less than a mediocre one, at equal position. The platform has every interest in showing what people click on.

The two figures that decide everything

Worked example

Budget: €1,000. Cost per click: €1.20 → about 833 visitors.

Conversion rate of the site: 2% → 17 customers.

Acquisition cost = 1,000 / 17 ≈ €59 per customer.

Profitable if a customer brings in more than €59 over their lifetime. Ruinous if they bring in €40 — and in that case, raising the budget only speeds up the losses.

The most effective lever is not in fact the cost per click, but the conversion rate: taking it from 2% to 3% brings the acquisition cost down to €39, without spending a euro more on advertising.

Where to invest, depending on the situation

  • Search (Google Ads) — the person is already expressing a need. Better conversion, higher cost.
  • Social networks (Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn) — you interrupt somebody who was not looking for anything. Cheaper, slower to convince.
  • Retargeting — showing an ad again to a visitor who already came. The best return, but quickly irritating: cap the frequency.
Common mistake — sending the ad to the home page

An ad for “women's trail running shoes” landing on the site's home page forces the visitor to search. Most leave.

Every ad must lead to a page that keeps its promise exactly. It is the cheapest and most profitable correction in online advertising.

💡 Before launching a campaign, know what a customer is worth. Without that figure you are not steering anything: you are spending.

📱 Social networks

2 min Social networks let you build an audience and a relationship.

Social networks let you build an audience and a relationship. In exchange they impose a dependency: the platform decides who sees your posts, and can change its rules overnight.

Choose rather than spread yourself thin

PlatformRelevant forDominant format
LinkedInSelling to businesses, recruitment, expertiseText, accounts of experience
InstagramVisual products, places, craftPhoto, short video
TikTokGeneral public, strong organic reachVertical video
YouTubeComplex subjects, lasting contentLong video

Being everywhere with the same recycled content gives a mediocre presence everywhere. One platform kept up properly produces more.

Organic reach is shrinking

Every platform follows the same trajectory: strong free visibility at launch to attract creators, then a gradual reduction in order to sell advertising. A page that reached half its followers ten years ago today reaches a small fraction.

The strategic consequence is clear: convert your audience into a channel you own — an email list, a website. An account can be suspended, a reach can collapse; a subscriber list stays.

What actually works

  • Regularity — two posts a week kept up for six months beat ten in one week and then nothing.
  • Replying to comments — platforms reward exchanges, and it is what creates a community.
  • Showing the workings — the failures and the process are more interesting than polished announcements.
  • One idea per post — a clear message travels better than a complete one.
Common mistake — chasing followers

An account with 50,000 inactive followers is worth less than one with 800 engaged ones. Prize draws attract great numbers of people who came for the prize and will never react again — and who, by dragging down your engagement rate, reduce the reach of your following posts.

💡 You are renting your audience from the platform. The only asset genuinely yours is the contact list you extract from it.

✉️ Email marketing

2 min Regarded as outdated, email remains the channel with the best return — for a simple reason: the list belongs to you .

Regarded as outdated, email remains the channel with the best return — for a simple reason: the list belongs to you. No algorithm stands between you and your subscribers.

Building an honest list

Consent must be explicit: a box ticked deliberately, never pre-filled. It is a legal obligation under data protection law, and above all the condition for it to work — a bought list generates spam reports in bulk and ruins your sender reputation for a long time.

A subscriber is earned through a clear exchange: useful content for an address, with a precise statement of what they will receive and how often.

The metrics that count

MetricWhat it reveals
Open rateThe quality of the subject line and trust in the sender
Click rateThe real relevance of the content — the most reliable metric
UnsubscribesA useful signal: too many sends, or a promise not kept
Spam reportsThe most serious: a handful is enough to degrade deliverability for all your sends

The open rate has become unreliable since some services preload images. The click rate remains the reference measure.

Segmentation

Worked example

A list of 5,000 subscribers receives the same message. Click rate: 1.8%, that is 90 clicks.

The same list split into three groups — new subscribers, recent customers, inactive for six months — with a message suited to each: click rate around 4%, that is 200 clicks.

Same list, same product, a little more writing work: the result doubles.

Common mistake — writing to everybody as though to nobody

“Dear customers, we are delighted to announce…”: a message that could be addressed to anybody interests nobody. Write to one specific person, with one specific need. The others will recognise themselves or not — and that is exactly as it should be.

One more point: a visible, working unsubscribe link beats a hidden one. Somebody who cannot find the link clicks “spam”, which costs you far more than an unsubscribe.

💡 A list of 500 people who deliberately gave you their address has more commercial value than 50,000 followers on a social network.

📚 Going further

  • Google Search Console — free, and indispensable: the real queries bringing visitors to you, your positions, and the errors to fix. To be installed before anything else.
  • Google Search Central — the official ranking documentation. Where it contradicts a blog, it is right.
  • Marketing and digital news sites that source their figures — worth more than the ones that repeat a press release without checking it.
  • Your national data protection authority's guidance on marketing — what the law actually allows for commercial email. Saves expensive mistakes.
  • “Building a StoryBrand”, Donald Miller — on how to phrase an offer so it is understood in five seconds. Short and directly applicable.

A note on method: before any advertising budget, measure your current conversion rate. Doubling that rate often costs less than doubling the traffic — and then benefits all your other channels.
