The path

Three levels, to be followed in order or picked up wherever you stand. Each reads on its own, but each assumes what came before.

  1. Level 1 Beginner Understanding the four basic levers. No prior knowledge needed.

    • Online advertising
    • Social networks
    • Email marketing

    4 sections · about 9 minutes · no prior knowledge needed

  2. Level 2 Intermediate Understanding where people leave, and why.
    • The buying journey and where it leaks

    • Relationship email and automated sequences

    4 sections · about 12 minutes · assumes level 1

  3. Level 3 Advanced What the tools do not measure, and what the law governs.
    • Attribution: who really gets credit for the sale
    • Retention, more profitable than acquisition
    • Persuading without manipulating
    • Outreach, cookies and advertising: the legal framework

    4 sections · about 13 minutes · assumes levels 1 and 2

  • Why four steps at 50% leave only 6% of visitors at the end.
  • How to check in one minute whether a piece of content stands a chance of ranking.
  • Which figures deserve a place on a dashboard, and which are decoration.

  • Exactly where the line runs between convincing and deceiving.

Before you start

Warning

This path is educational. The rates quoted are orders of magnitude meant to convey a mechanism: they vary enormously by sector, price and audience.

It is not legal advice. Collecting addresses is governed by data protection law — the GDPR in Europe, equivalent rules elsewhere: check what applies to you with the official sources before building a list.
